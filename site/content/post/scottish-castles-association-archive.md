James Grimster writes:

> Today we are launching the **Scottish Castles Association Archive**, a unique resource for exploring the rich and varied history and architecture of Scotland's castles, towers and fortified houses.

You can explore the archive now on the University of Stirling collections website at [www.stirlingcollections.org.uk/scottish-castles-association-archive](https://www.stirlingcollections.org.uk/scottish-castles-association-archive/).

![Scottish Castles Association Archive on the University of Stirling collections website](/img/sca_gb559.jpg)

## A national resource for Scotland's castles

The Scottish Castles Association Archive is a partnership between the Scottish Castles Association and the University of Stirling. It brings together records, photographs, plans and research on Scotland's castles, from ruined towers to fortified houses that have been adapted and lived in for centuries. It is for researchers, owners, local communities and anyone with an interest in how these buildings came to look the way they do.

## Powered by CollectionsBase

The archive is published on the University of Stirling collections website, which runs on our **CollectionsBase** platform. CollectionsBase presents data from two systems:

- **Axiell CALM** and **Axiell Collections**

## A new cluster map of geocoded archive records

The launch also introduces a **cluster map of geocoded archive records**. Castles are tied to place, so the map lets you start from the landscape rather than from a search term.

- Records are plotted by location across Scotland.
- Nearby records group into clusters, which you can zoom in on to see individual sites.
- You can move from a place on the map to the archive records for it.

This builds on the geocoded data held in the catalogue. Archives with a strong sense of place can use it to give visitors another way into their collections.

## Congratulations to everyone involved

Congratulations to the Scottish Castles Association and the University of Stirling Archives on the launch. We are proud to support it. [Explore the Scottish Castles Association Archive](https://www.stirlingcollections.org.uk/scottish-castles-association-archive/) today.
